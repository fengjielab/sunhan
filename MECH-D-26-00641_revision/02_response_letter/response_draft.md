# Response to the Editor and Reviewers

Manuscript: `MECH-D-26-00641`

Dear Editor and Reviewers,

Thank you for the detailed and constructive assessment. We agree that the submitted
three-participant study did not isolate the contributions of adaptive master haptics
and adaptive gripper execution, and that its block-level inferential analysis did not
respect participant clustering. We have therefore designed a new confirmatory,
participant-level 2x2 ablation study and have revised the acquisition system to retain
online perception, parameter-transition, haptic-command, gripper, phase, and timing
records. The submitted 135-trial study will be presented only as exploratory evidence.

This file is intentionally a controlled draft. Exact sample sizes, estimates,
confidence intervals, p-values, figure/table numbers, repository DOI, ethics details,
and page/line locations must be inserted only after formal data collection and final
manuscript compilation. Do not use placeholder numbers in the submitted response.

## Major changes to report after completion

1. New four-condition experiment: `I`, `I_H`, `I_G`, and `I_H_G`.
2. Eight operators, balanced randomized crossover order, two sessions, and a
   participant-level mixed-effects analysis.
3. Neutral disabled-channel baseline and locked parameter/model provenance.
4. Online visual latency and trigger logs, phase-specific timing, haptic commands,
   external-force estimates, gripper aperture/requests, saturation and loop jitter.
5. Narrowed claims and explicit limits regarding direct grasp force, deformation,
   passivity, generalization, and the predefined lookup mapping.

## Response-writing rule

For each point, use the following four-part structure:

- **Response:** agree/disagree with a precise technical reason.
- **Action:** state the experiment, analysis, or wording change actually completed.
- **Evidence:** give exact estimates and uncertainty without overstating causality.
- **Location:** cite final page, section, table/figure, and line numbers.

The authoritative point list and current completion state are maintained in
`../01_review_materials/review_response_matrix.md`.
