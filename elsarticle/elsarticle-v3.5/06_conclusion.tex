\section{Conclusion}
This study presented an object-conditioned strategy-level parameter-configuration framework that coordinates slave-side impedance, master-side haptic feedback, and gripper execution. The framework uses an intermediate object-to-strategy mapping to coordinate a one-shot, strategy-locked parameter deployment based on asynchronous visual inference.

Across 27 matched blocks (135 trials), complete multi-channel configuration reduced mean task completion time by 1.79 s (8.5\%; bootstrap 95\% CI, 1.10--2.51 s) compared with impedance-only reconfiguration. The direction of this difference was consistent across all three operators and all six tested objects. Secondary descriptive outcomes (success rate and Raw NASA-TLX) also favored the complete configuration, whereas the trajectory-length confidence interval included zero.

These findings support a within-sample, bundled system-level benefit of coordinating the three parameter channels under the tested conditions. Individual channel contributions remain to be isolated through ablation experiments.
