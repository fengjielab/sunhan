## Suggested citation granularity in the working manuscript

- Cite code provenance once at the end of the system/configuration subsection and once at the realized-intervention subsection, using a repository/archive citation only if a stable public or supplementary code record is created.
- Cite the clean-analysis provenance in the data-cleaning subsection through a supplementary reproducibility statement rather than exposing local filesystem paths in the submitted paper.
- Keep all `[NEEDS VERIFICATION]` markers in the working draft until documentary evidence is supplied. They must not appear as unresolved assertions in the submitted manuscript.
