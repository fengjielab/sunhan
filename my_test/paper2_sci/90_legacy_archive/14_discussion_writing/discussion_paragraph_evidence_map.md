## Classification rule used in the draft

- **Data-supported:** can be traced verbatim or numerically to frozen Results/Methods.
- **Interpretation:** explains the joint meaning of supported findings while maintaining association language.
- **Speculation:** proposes a possible mechanism not directly measured and is explicitly qualified.
