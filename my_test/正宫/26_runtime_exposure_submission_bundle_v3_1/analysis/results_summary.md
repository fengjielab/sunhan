# V3.1 human-variability stress-test results

The locked cohort remained 20 independent participants, 300 planned trials, 294 evaluable trials, and 6 safety aborts.

Recorded-state classification was 294/294 (100%; exact 95% CI 98.75%–100%). Timing MAE/P95/max were 2.381/4.957/5.408 ms; exposure MAE/P95/max were 0.001798/0.005996/0.006760.

Participant-mean approach duration ranged 1.3033–2.8360 s, Omega path 0.02367–0.04643 m, Panda path 0.00954–0.02444 m, peak speed 0.02677–0.03421 m/s, internal-force impulse 0.2492–1.1881 N·s, and whole-trial clamp rate 6.67%–78.57%.

Every participant classified at 100%. The worst participant timing MAE used 15.26% of the 20-ms limit and the worst exposure MAE used 12.61% of the 0.02 limit. Within the observed variability envelope, no participant-level criterion failure was observed; this is not proof of invariance outside the sample.

Spearman coefficients and participant-resampled bootstrap intervals are descriptive. No p-values or classification associations were computed. Physical delivery was not independently observed, and human force outcomes remain exploratory.
