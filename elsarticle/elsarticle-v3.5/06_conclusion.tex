\section{Conclusion}
This study developed an object-conditioned strategy-level parameter-bundle framework for haptic teleoperation of heterogeneous objects. The framework maps detected object classes to bounded operating policies that configure slave-side impedance, master-side haptic rendering, and gripper execution as a coordinated parameter bundle. An asynchronous one-shot update integrates low-rate visual perception with the nominal 200-Hz supervisory loop while locking the selected strategy during each trial.

Across 27 matched task blocks and 135 physical trials, the complete bundle reduced mean completion time by 1.79 s compared with impedance-only reconfiguration, corresponding to an 8.5\% reduction (bootstrap 95\% CI, 1.10--2.51 s). The difference directionally favored the complete bundle across all three operators and six objects. The complete bundle also showed the highest observed success rate and the lowest Raw NASA-TLX score, although these secondary outcomes were descriptive.

These findings should be interpreted as within-sample, system-level evidence under the tested conditions, while channel-level ablations and broader evaluations are needed to isolate individual contributions and assess generalizability.
